# Plan: Art Assets for Printable Prototype
## Unit Discs, Commander Cards, and Flexible Setup

---

## Confirmed Decisions

- **Play modes:** Co-op (2vAI), 1v1, 2v2 — same core ruleset
- **Commanders locked to own units** (no cross-faction recruitment in final play; cross-faction allowed only for testing)
- **5 unit types per commander** (equal pool size for all commanders)
- **Army size options:** 6, 8, or 10 units per side (including commander)
- **Deck construction:** 1 random card per squad type + 1 random commander card + fill from chosen pools (all draws random/hidden)
- **Commander cards:** 10 unique designs per commander, 2 drawn randomly for deck
- **Unit-type cards:** 5 designs per type, 1 card per squad type drawn
- **Unit discs:** 2" diameter, colored border by commander

## Deck Construction (All Modes)

### Step 1: Army Selection
- Choose 1 commander
- Choose 5, 7, or 9 additional units (same count for all sides)
- Must field **at least 3 different unit types** among the 6/8/10 total
- Units drawn only from commander's own pool

### Step 2: Deck Building (10 / 13 / 16 cards)
All draws are random. Cards are not revealed during setup.

| Step | Draw | Count | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2a | Unit cards | 1 per squad type | Random from each unit type's pool |
| 2b | Commander cards | 1 | Random from commander's 10-card pool |
| 2c | Fill to target | Remainder | Player selects 2 source pools; draw random from those pools |

**Example (Small/10 cards):** David brings Swordsman, Swordsman, Spearman, Slinger, Scout
- Step 2a: 1 random Swordsman card, 1 random Spearman card, 1 random Slinger card, 1 random Scout card = 4 cards
- Step 2b: 1 random David card = 1 card
- Step 2c: 5 more cards. Player chooses 2 pools (e.g., "David cards" + "Swordsman cards"). Draw 5 random cards from combined pool.

### Step 3: Starting Hands
- Shuffle deck
- Deal **4 cards** to each player
- Remaining deck face-down

### Formation Card Rule
- Each unit-type pool has exactly **1 double-formation card** (top = Formation A, bottom = Formation B)
- Other 4 cards are standard attack/move/utility
- Total per unit type = 5 cards

1. **Finalize unit-type card text** for all 15 types (5 per commander)
2. **Finalize commander card text** for all 4 commanders (10 each)
3. **Generate art assets:**
   - Phase 1: 15 disc portraits + card back template
   - Phase 2: 40 commander card fronts
   - Phase 3: 75 unit-type card fronts
4. **Print and playtest** with 6-unit armies
5. **Iterate balance** based on playtest feedback
6. **Scale to 8/10-unit armies** if 6-unit feels too constrained
