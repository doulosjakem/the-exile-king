# Abner — Command Cards

> **Unit Type:** Commander
> **Faction:** Saul's Kingdom
> **Availability:** MVP (enemy/scenario)

---

## Formation Card: ABNER'S COMMAND / HOST'S CRY

### TOP ACTION — Abner's Command

**Activations:** 3

Issue an order to all units of one type within 2 spaces of Abner:
Those units may command immediately this turn.

**Purpose:**
Concentrate force on a critical sector.

---

### BOTTOM ACTION — Host's Cry

**Activations:** 2

All Saul's Kingdom units within 2 spaces of Abner gain +1 Attack this turn.

**Purpose:**
Boost the entire front line simultaneously.

## Notes

- Abner is the central command hub for Saul's Kingdom.
- His formation cards affect all nearby units of one type — very powerful for focused attacks.
- If Abner is eliminated, Saul's Kingdom loses 1 Activation from their command pool for the rest of the scenario.
- Elite Bodyguards are specifically designed to protect Abner.
