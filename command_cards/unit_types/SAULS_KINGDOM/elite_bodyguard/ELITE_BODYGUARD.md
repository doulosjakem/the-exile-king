# Elite Bodyguard — Command Cards

> **Unit Type:** Elite (Protect Saul/Abner)
> **Faction:** Saul's Kingdom
> **Availability:** MVP (enemy/scenario)

---

## Formation Card: GUARD / SHIELD BROTHER

### TOP ACTION — Guard

**Activations:** 2

Elite Bodyguard may move adjacent to a Commander (Saul or Abner).
Until start of next turn, the Commander gains 2 Shield when Elite Bodyguards are adjacent.

**Purpose:**
Anchor protection around the commander.

---

### BOTTOM ACTION — Shield Brother

**Activations:** 3

When an adjacent enemy attacks the Commander,
the Elite Bodyguard may intercept:
take the damage instead.

The Elite Bodyguard is eliminated after intercepting.

**Purpose:**
Shield the commander from harm.

## Notes

- Elite Bodyguards exist solely to protect the Commander.
- Shield Brother is the signature ability — sacrifices the Bodyguard to save the Commander. Bodyguards are lost after intercepting.
- Guard positions Bodyguards as a shield wall around the command unit.
- Without the Commander, Bodyguards are just above-average melee units.
- If the Commander is eliminated, Bodyguards lose their special abilities.
