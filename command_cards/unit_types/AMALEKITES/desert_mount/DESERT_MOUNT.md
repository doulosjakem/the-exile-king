# Desert Mount — Command Cards

> **Unit Type:** Unique (Mobile heavy — fast mounted raiders)
> **Faction:** Amalekites
> **Availability:** MVP (enemy)

---

## Formation: DESERT CHARGE / SAND DASH

### TOP ACTION — Desert Charge

**Activations:** 3

Desert Mount may move 4 spaces and attack.

Gain +2 Attack for this attack.

If the attack defeats an enemy,
may move 2 additional spaces.

**Purpose:**
Devastating deep strike that ignores the battlefield.

---

### BOTTOM ACTION — Sand Dash

**Activations:** 2

Desert Mount may move 3 spaces.

All enemy units within 1 space of the Desert Mount's path suffer 1 damage.

Ignores terrain penalties during this move.

**Purpose:**
Mobile area denial that cuts through enemy lines.

## Notes

- Desert Mounts are the Amalekite unique unit — fast, hard-hitting, and mobile.
- Desert Charge is the premier offensive card — speed + power + pursuit on the enemy's terms.
- Sand Dash leverages fast movement for area denial while ignoring terrain.
- Mount represents a fast desert horse or donkey, not a camel — camels were not used as war mounts by Amalekites in this period.
- These units are effective across all desert map types and ignore terrain penalties.
