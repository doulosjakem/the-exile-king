# Jonathan's Followers — Command Cards

> **Faction Philosophy:** Small temporary allied force. Fanatically loyal, disciplined, and precise.

---

## Faction Overview

Jonathan's Men — a small allied force that supports David in specific scenarios. Available as a playable faction in the prototype.
