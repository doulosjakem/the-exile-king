# David's Company — Command Cards

> **Faction Philosophy:** Flexible underdog warband. Core mechanics revolve around the Command Card deck system, activation economy, and adaptability.

---

## Faction Overview

David's warband of refugees, outcasts, and loyal fighters. The only faction with the Command Card deck system.

Available in **Prototype**.

---

| Unit | Type | Notes |

---

## Deck Construction

- Build a deck of exactly **10 cards** for Small army size.
- Draw **1 card per unit type** brought (random from that type's pool).
- Draw **1 random David card**.
- Fill remaining cards by drawing from **2 chosen pools** (David + 1 unit type).
- Reshuffle if duplicate commanders are drawn.

---

## Turn Structure

1. Draw 2 cards from deck → hand (hand size grows over time).
2. Choose 1 card to play this turn, place 1 card face-down as reserve.
3. Reveal all cards simultaneously.
4. Resolve in Initiative order (highest first):
   - **Top** of one card + **Bottom** of the other card.
5. Spent cards go to Spent pile.
6. **Fatigue:** Lose 1 random card from hand → Lost pile after each turn.

---

## Card Loss

- **Casualty:** When the last unit of a type is eliminated, remove 1 matching card from deck → Lost pile.

---

## Army Building (Prototype)

- Select exactly **1 commander**.
- Select additional units to reach **6 total** (including commander).
- Must include **at least 3 different unit types**.
- All units drawn only from this faction's roster.
