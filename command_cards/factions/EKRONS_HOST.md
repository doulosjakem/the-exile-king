# Lord of Ekron's Host — Command Cards

> **Faction Philosophy:** Fast assault with chariots and slingers. Coastal plain warfare — mobile, aggressive, and overwhelming in open terrain.

---

## Faction Overview

Lord of Ekron's Host. A mobile Philistine force specializing in chariot raids and slinger harassment. Strong in open terrain, vulnerable in rough ground.

Playable in **Prototype**.
